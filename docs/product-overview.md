# AGIS - Product Overview



**AI Agent Governance & Inventory System**



## 1. What is AGIS?



AGIS is an early-stage enterprise AI governance platform designed to help organizations maintain visibility, accountability, and oversight of AI agents.



It aims to provide a centralized place to register AI agents, identify their human owners, review their risks, and manage governance workflows.



**Vision:** Become a centralized governance layer between AI innovation and enterprise operations.



## 2. Why AGIS?



AI agents are increasingly capable of interacting with business tools, APIs, databases, and internal systems.



As adoption grows, organizations need answers to important questions:



- Which AI agents are operating in our environment?

- Who is responsible for each agent?

- What tools and permissions does an agent have?

- Has the agent passed the required review?

- What governance actions have been performed?

- Which agents require additional attention?



AGIS is being developed to address these governance challenges.



## 3. Core Product Areas



### AI Agent Inventory

A centralized registry for recording and viewing AI agents.



### Ownership and Accountability

A governance model built around identifiable human responsibility for AI agents.



### Risk Assessment

A foundation for classifying and reviewing agent-related risks.



### Approval Workflow

A role-based review process with approval and rejection decisions.



### Access Governance

Admin, Reviewer, and Viewer roles with backend authorization.



### Auditability

Records selected governance and operational actions.



### Operational Status

Provides application-level management of recorded agent status.



## 4. Intended Users



- Enterprise IT and technology teams.

- AI platform and engineering teams.

- Security and governance professionals.

- Risk and compliance teams.

- Organizations adopting AI agents at scale.



These are intended user groups; AGIS has not yet established validated enterprise customers.



## 5. Product Differentiation



AGIS is being developed around a registration-first governance approach.



Rather than focusing exclusively on model traffic or token usage, the product vision emphasizes:



- Agent identity and inventory.

- Human accountability.

- Governance lifecycle.

- Review and approval workflows.

- Organization-level separation.

- Traceable governance actions.



The long-term objective is to complement existing AI infrastructure and security tools, not to claim that AGIS replaces them.



## 6. Development Status



The current prototype demonstrates core inventory, authentication, authorization, tenant isolation, and governance workflows.



The following remain future development areas:



- Automated Shadow AI discovery.

- Runtime monitoring.

- Automated enforcement.

- Credential revocation and actual agent termination.

- Broader enterprise integrations.

- Production deployment and independent security validation.



## 7. Long-Term Vision



As organizations move toward increasingly autonomous AI systems, governance must evolve alongside innovation.



AGIS aims to help organizations understand what AI agents exist, who is accountable for them, and how their use is governed.
